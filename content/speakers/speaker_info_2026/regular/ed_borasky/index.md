### xen.assist: Assisted Xentonal Algorithmic Composition with R
Regular talk, 9:40 - 10:40 AM

Inspired by the works of Erv Wilson (Narushima 2019) and William Sethares (Sethares 2013), xen.assist is an R package for composers of xentonal electronic music - music in scales other than the traditional 12-tone equal divisions of the octave. Based on previous packages eikosany (Borasky 2026b) and consonaR (Borasky 2026a), xen.assist features:

* Scale generation and keyboard mapping (Wilson),
* Scale, timbre and consonance analysis (Sethares), and
* Agentic AI assistance for the composition process.

The talk will focus on the latter - the use of agentic AI, enabled by R packages that interact with large language models. And there will be musical samples!

References:

* Borasky, M. Edward (Ed). 2026a. consonaR: Consonance-Based Algorithmic Composition. https://algocompsynth.github.io/consonaR/.
* Borasky, M. Edward (Ed). 2026b. Eikosany: Algorithmic Composition with Erv Wilson’s Combination Product Sets. https://algocompsynth.github.io/eikosany/.
* Narushima, T. 2019. Microtonality and the Tuning Systems of Erv Wilson. Routledge Studies in Music Theory. Taylor & Francis Limited.
* Sethares, W. A. 2013. Tuning, Timbre, Spectrum, Scale, Second Edition. Springer London.
